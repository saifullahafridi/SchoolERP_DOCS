# Transport, Library and Notice board

## Transport

Open **School → Transport**. Every staff member can look at it (so the office can answer a parent's call); only Admin
and Principal can change it. It has three tabs: **Routes**, **Riders** and **Vehicles**.

The usual order is: add the vehicles → add a route with its stops → add the students who ride it. After that the
transport fee goes on each rider's monthly fee voucher by itself.

### Vehicles

Click **Add vehicle** and enter the **Registration no.**, number of **Seats**, the **Driver** and the driver's mobile.
The model, driver's CNIC and an attendant (with mobile) are optional. A vehicle that no longer runs can be marked
inactive (untick **Vehicle in use**). A vehicle that runs a route cannot be deleted until the route gets another
vehicle.

### Routes and stops

1. On the **Routes** tab click **Add route**.
2. Enter the **Route name** and pick the **Vehicle**.
3. Add the stops in the order the van reaches them: the stop name, **Pick-up** and **Drop** time, and the
   **Monthly fee** a student from that stop pays. Use **Add stop** for more.
4. Click **Save**.

Each route card shows the vehicle and driver, how many riders it has against the seats (the bar turns red when there
are more riders than seats), every stop with its riders and fee, and the route's total each month.

- A stop that students still ride from cannot be removed. Move them to another stop first.
- A stop or route that students have ridden in the past is kept for their fee history: mark the route inactive
  (untick **Route in use**) instead of deleting it.

**Route sheet** opens a page to print for the driver and attendant: every stop in order with its time, and under
each stop the students waiting there with their class, guardian, mobile and address, plus Am / Pm boxes to tick.

### Riders

The **Riders** tab lists the students on school transport this month, with their route and stop, pick-up time,
monthly fee, the month they started and their guardian. Pick a route or search a name to narrow the list.

**Add a rider**

1. Click **Add rider** and type the student's name or admission no.
2. Choose the **Route**, the **Stop** and the **From month** (the first month the fee is charged).
3. Leave **Monthly fee** empty to charge the stop's fee, or type another amount if the school agreed one.
4. Click **Save**. If the route now has more riders than seats, the app tells you (it is still saved).

**Change stop:** click **Change** on the rider and choose the new stop and the month it starts. The old stop is charged
up to the month before, so past vouchers still show what was charged and why.

**Stop transport:** click **End** and choose the **Last month** the fee is charged. **Entered by mistake? Remove**
deletes the entry, but only if no voucher has charged it yet.

A month that is already billed cannot be changed: cancel that voucher first, or make the change from the next month.

### The transport fee on vouchers

The first route you add makes a monthly fee head called **Transport** (see [Fee setup](05-fees.md#fee-setup)). When
you [generate the month's vouchers](05-fees.md#make-the-months-vouchers-generate-vouchers), riders get a Transport line
with their own monthly fee. Students who don't ride get no Transport line (unless the class has a Transport amount in
the fee structure). Concessions and the sibling discount on the Transport head work as for any other fee.

### What parents see

In the [Parent Portal](09-parent-portal.md), a child who rides school transport has a **Transport** tab: the route and
stop, pick-up and drop times, the monthly fee, the vehicle, and the driver's and attendant's phone numbers (tap to
call).

## Library

Open **Academics → Library**. Every staff member can look up books and loans; Admin, Principal and teachers run the
desk and add books; only Admin and Principal change the library rules and waive fines. The cards at the top show the
number of titles, books on loan, overdue books and fines owed (click one to see the list). The page has three tabs:
**Desk**, **Loans** and **Books**.

### Library rules

Click **Library rules** (Admin / Principal) to set how many days a student and a staff member keep a book, how many
books each may have at a time, the fine for each late day and the most fine on one book (0 = no limit). The rules start
at 14 days and 2 books for students, 30 days and 5 books for staff, and a fine of 10 a day up to 500.

### Adding books

1. On the **Books** tab click **Add book**.
2. Enter the **Title**. Author, category, shelf, publisher, ISBN and price are optional. The **Price** is what a
   borrower pays if they lose a copy.
3. Enter how many **Copies** the library has. Each copy gets a number, from the next free number, or from the
   **First book no.** you type (useful when your register already has numbers).
4. Click **Save**. The app tells you the numbers given; write each one inside its copy.

**Copies** on a book's row lists every copy with its number, whether it is on the shelf, on loan (and with whom) or lost.
From there you can add more copies, **Withdraw** a worn-out copy, or put a lost copy that was found **Back on shelf**.
A book that was never lent can be deleted; one that was lent is kept for the loan history.

### Lending a book (Desk)

1. On the **Desk** tab type the **Book no.** written in the book and click **Find**.
2. Choose **Student** or **Staff** and type a name. The app shows how many books they have, and any overdue book or
   fine.
3. Check the **Return by** date (it comes from the library rules) and click **Lend**.

Nobody with an overdue book can borrow another, and nobody can go over their number of books.

### Taking a book back

Type the book's number on the **Desk** and click **Find**. The app shows who has it, when it was due, and the fine if it
comes back today.

- **Take back:** the book is back on the shelf today. A late book gets its fine.
- **Renew:** the borrower keeps it for another loan period from its return date (up to 3 times; not when it is
  already late).
- **Other date or lost…:** take it back on an earlier date, or tick **The book was lost**. A lost book costs its price
  (or the amount you type) plus any late fine.

The **Loans** tab lists books **On loan**, **Overdue**, **Fines owed**, **Returned** or **All**, with the same buttons.
**Undo** removes a loan made by mistake.

### Fines

A fine can be settled in three ways:

- **Paid** at the library (any desk user).
- **Waive** (Admin / Principal).
- Do nothing: a student's fine goes by itself onto their next fee voucher, as a **Library fine** line, when you
  [generate vouchers](05-fees.md#make-the-months-vouchers-generate-vouchers). It is then paid with the fees. If that
  voucher is cancelled, the fine goes onto the next one again.

A staff member's fine is paid or waived at the library.

### What parents see

In the [Parent Portal](09-parent-portal.md), a child who has borrowed books has a **Library** tab: each book, the date
it is due back (late ones in red), and any fine with whether it is paid, waived or on a voucher.

## Notice board

Open **Overview → Notice board** to post circulars. Admin and Principal post them; other staff can read them.

1. Click **Post notice**.
2. Enter the **Title** and the **Notice**, and choose **For**: staff, parents or both.
3. A notice for parents can be for some classes only. Tick the **Classes**, or tick none for every class.
4. Choose **Publish on** and, if you like, **Expires on**. A notice can be pinned to stay on top and can carry an
   attachment.
5. Click **Post**.

Staff see live notices on their Dashboard. Parents see the notices for their children's classes on the portal's first
page, and the school sees how many parents have read each one (**Seen by**).

A parents' notice can also be texted: choose **Send by** (SMS or WhatsApp) and edit the **Message**. One text goes to
each mobile number, even if a family has several children.
