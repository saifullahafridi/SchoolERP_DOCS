# School Groups (Branches) and Transfers

An owner with several branches can join them in one **School Group**. The Owner (Super Admin) makes the group and puts
the branches in it. Each branch still keeps its own students, fees and staff.

## One login for all branches

A group login (Admin, Principal or Accountant of the group) first opens the group pages:

- **All branches:** students, attendance and fees of every branch side by side.
- **Fees across branches:** collections and defaulters of all branches together.
- **Group sign-ins:** make group logins (for example a head office accountant). Admin only.

To work inside one branch, pick it from the branch switcher at the top of the sidebar. The whole app then works for
that branch. Pick the group again to go back.

## Move a student or staff member to another branch (Branch transfers)

1. Open **Branch transfers** and click **Move student** or **Move staff member**.
2. Choose the From branch, the person, the To branch, the date and (for a student) the new class / section.
3. The app shows what the student still owes at the old branch. You must agree to leave these dues there, or clear
   them first.
4. Click **Move student** (or **Move staff member**) to finish.

What happens:

- **Student:** the old record is marked Left, and a new record with a new admission no. is made at the new branch.
  Unpaid vouchers for months after the move are cancelled at the old branch. The photo is copied.
- **Parent login:** the parent's Portal login moves with the child. If a brother or sister stays at the old branch,
  the parent can see both branches and switch between them in the Portal.
- **Staff:** the old record becomes inactive and stops being a class teacher. Their login and salary move to the new
  branch. A teacher who still has lessons on the old branch's timetable cannot be moved until those lessons are given
  to someone else.

The student's page at both branches shows a note about the transfer.
