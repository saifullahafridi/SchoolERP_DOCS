Fees

Make the month's vouchers in one click. Collect at the counter in seconds.

Set your fee heads and class-wise amounts once. Each month, generate vouchers for the whole school with a due date and late fine.

  • Type a voucher no., admission no., name or family no. like F-12 at the counter and take the payment
  • Sibling discount applied automatically, for example 20% off tuition for the 2nd child
  • Defaulter list by months unpaid, and SMS or WhatsApp reminders joined into one message per family
  • Running vouchers twice is safe: students who already have one are skipped
Online payments

JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfers, checked in one place.

Parents pay into your school's account, then send the transaction ID and a photo of the receipt from the portal. Your accountant approves or rejects it, and the parent gets an SMS either way.

  • Approved payments go straight onto the voucher, with the TID as reference
  • A red badge in the menu shows how many proofs are waiting
  • One proof can cover all of a parent's children
Attendance

Parents know the same day when a child is absent.

Teachers mark Present, Absent, Late or Leave for their own sections. Fifteen minutes later, parents of absent children get an SMS. If a teacher fixes a mistake before then, nothing is sent.

  • Monthly grid for each section, plus staff attendance and leave
  • Pakistan's national holidays added in one click; attendance is closed on holidays
  • Text all parents about a new holiday

Exams, timetable and homework

Marks in, report cards out.

Teachers enter marks for their own subjects. When all marks are in, publish the exam: totals, percentage, grade and class position are worked out, and parents see the report card in the portal.

  • Timetable by class and by teacher, with each teacher's workload
  • Daily homework and diary that parents can read
  • School Leaving, Character and Bonafide certificates
  • Promote the whole school at the end of the year
Parent portal

Everything a parent asks the office, on their phone.

Each parent gets a login, usually their mobile number. They see fees due, attendance, homework, results and the timetable for each child, and can send a payment proof without coming to school.

  • One login covers brothers and sisters, even at different branches
  • Upcoming holidays on the home page
  • Works in any phone browser
Staff, payroll and expenses

Payslips each month, and a clear view of income and spending.

Set each person's basic pay and allowances once. Generate the month's payslips, cut absent days if you choose, and recover salary advances in instalments.

  • Staff see their own payslips and apply for leave from their login
  • Record electricity, rent, repairs and other expenses
  • Fee income beside salaries and expenses, month by month
More than one branch

All your branches under one owner login.

Join your campuses in a School Group. Each branch keeps its own students, fees and staff, while head office sees them side by side.

  • Students, attendance and fees of every branch on one page
  • Collections and defaulters across all branches together
  • Move a student or teacher to another branch; the parent's login follows the child

Common questions

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Every page works in a phone browser, for staff and for parents.

Can other schools see our data?

No. Each school sees only its own data. Branches in a group are seen together only by your group logins.

What if we change a fee in the middle of the year?

Change it in Fee setup. Vouchers already made keep their amounts; new vouchers use the new amount.

A parent forgot their password.

The school office resets it. The parent chooses a new one at their next sign-in.
